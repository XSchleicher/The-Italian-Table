Join Our Team

The Italian Table is always looking for hardworking and dedicated individuals to become part of our team. We strive to create a welcoming workplace where employees can work together and provide excellent service to our customers.

Why Work With Us?

At The Italian Table, we value teamwork, respect, and a positive work environment. We provide opportunities for employees to develop new skills, gain experience in the restaurant industry, and grow within the company.

Available Positions

Restaurant Manager

Restaurant managers are responsible for overseeing daily operations and making sure each location runs smoothly. They work with employees to provide excellent customer service, maintain quality standards, and create a positive dining experience.

Chef / Cook

Chefs and cooks are responsible for preparing delicious meals while maintaining the quality of our food. They work as part of a team to keep the kitchen organized, follow food safety standards, and ensure that every dish meets our expectations.

Server

Servers are responsible for welcoming customers, taking orders, and providing friendly and helpful service. They help create an enjoyable dining experience by making sure customers are satisfied with their meals and overall visit.

Host / Hostess

Hosts and hostesses are responsible for greeting customers and helping them feel welcome when they arrive. They manage seating arrangements, assist with reservations, and help maintain an organized and welcoming dining area.

How to Apply

Interested in joining our team? Applicants must submit their resume in person at one of The Italian Table's locations for consideration. We look forward to meeting individuals who share our passion for great food, teamwork, and excellent customer service.
