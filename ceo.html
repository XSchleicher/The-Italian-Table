Michael Erickson

Chief Executive Officer

Giving Vision and Direction

Biography

Michael Erickson is the Chief Executive Officer of The Italian Table Inc. He is responsible for setting the overall vision and direction of the company while making sure the team stays focused on its goals. Michael works closely with the other members of leadership to help the business grow and provide customers with a great experience.

Leadership Style

Michael has a vision-focused leadership style. He encourages the team to work toward common goals while giving employees the direction they need to succeed.

Known for saying: "A strong vision gives the whole team somewhere to go."
