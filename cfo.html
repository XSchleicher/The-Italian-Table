Biography

Jane Warren is the Chief Financial Officer of The Italian Table Inc. She manages the company's finances and helps make responsible decisions about spending and growth. Jane works with the leadership team to make sure the business remains financially stable while continuing to improve.

Leadership Style

Jane has a careful and practical leadership style. She focuses on making responsible financial decisions while supporting the restaurant's long-term goals.

Known for saying: "Good decisions today create opportunities for tomorrow."
