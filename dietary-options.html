Dining With Dietary Needs

At The Italian Table, we want every guest to feel comfortable asking questions about the food they order. Whether you have a food allergy, dietary restriction, or ingredient concern, our team is here to help you make informed dining decisions.

Vegetarian Choices

Guests looking for vegetarian choices can ask our staff which menu items may meet their needs and whether modifications are available. Our menu includes a variety of Italian-inspired dishes featuring vegetables, pasta, and cheese.

Gluten-Related Questions

If you avoid gluten, please ask staff about ingredients and preparation methods before ordering. Pasta, bread, sauces, and other prepared ingredients may contain gluten. We cannot guarantee that a dish is gluten-free unless its ingredients and preparation procedures have been verified.

Food Allergy Guide

Knowing what goes into your meal is important. Some ingredients may not be immediately obvious, so here are several common allergens to be aware of when exploring our menu.

Dairy

Milk, cheese, butter, and cream may be found in pasta sauces, pizzas, desserts, and other Italian-inspired dishes.

Gluten

Wheat and gluten may be present in pasta, bread, breading, and certain sauces or prepared ingredients.

Tree Nuts & Peanuts

Nuts may be present in certain ingredients or prepared foods. Cross-contact is also a possibility during food preparation.

Eggs

Eggs may be ingredients in certain pastas, breading, baked goods, desserts, and sauces.

Shellfish

Shellfish may be present in seafood dishes or through shared preparation equipment and cooking areas.

Soy

Soy may be found in certain sauces, processed ingredients, and prepared foods used in restaurant dishes.

Your Safety Is Our Priority

Your health and safety matter to us. If you have a food allergy, please inform a team member before ordering. Our staff can help identify ingredients and discuss available preparation accommodations. Please be aware that cross-contact may occur, and we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free of allergens.

Before Placing Your Order

Always let your server know about any food allergies or dietary restrictions. Ask about ingredients, sauces, toppings, and preparation methods. Please confirm all allergy-related information directly with the restaurant before ordering.

Questions Before You Visit?

Have a question about ingredients or dietary options? We encourage you to reach out before your visit. You can also find information about our restaurant locations on our Locations page.
